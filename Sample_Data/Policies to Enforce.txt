Policies to Enforce:

Meal Per-Diem Limit

Meal expenses must not exceed ₹2,500 per day.

Flag any meal expense above this amount.

Lodging Nightly Limit

Hotel stays must not exceed ₹8,000 per night.

If the stay is longer than 5 nights, a clear business justification must be present.

Receipt Requirement

All expenses above ₹1,000 must include an itemized receipt.

Lodging and airfare expenses always require receipts, regardless of amount.

Classification Consistency

Expenses marked as Personal should not come from business vendors such as airlines, hotels, or taxis.

Expenses marked as Professional should not include personal-type vendors such as spas, salons, or clothing stores.

Duplicate Expenses

No two expenses should have the same employee name, date, merchant, and amount.

Flag duplicates as potential resubmissions.

Currency and Exchange Validation

Expenses paid abroad must be in local currency.

Exchange rates must not deviate by more than ±5% from the official daily rate.

Pre-Approval Check

Any single expense above ₹20,000 requires pre-approval.

Conferences, training, and international flights also require pre-approval regardless of cost.

Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following categories are never reimbursable: fines, minibar charges, alcohol-only receipts, parking violations, childcare, personal clothing, and lost item replacements.

Project / Cost Center Consistency

Expenses tied to a project code must only be in these categories: travel, lodging, meals, and equipment rental.

Total expenses per project must not exceed ₹5,00,000.

Unusual / Anomalous Spending

Flag any single expense that is more than 3 times the average amount for that employee in the same category.

These should be reviewed as potential anomalies.
